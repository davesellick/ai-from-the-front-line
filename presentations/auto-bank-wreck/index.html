Wrapping up · spotlight

Spotlight: auto bank rec.

Xero’s JAX aims to auto-reconcile 80%+ of statement lines, hands-free and in real time.

Wrapping up · spotlight

Why you shouldn’t turn it on.

It makes mistakes by design.
It can only be ‘probably’ confident.

It reconciles items without documentation or rules.

The human is an afterthought: a review at the end.

If everything needs reviewing, what’s the point?

Wrapping up · spotlight

How it could work.

A cash coding style screen. Review all suggested codings before pushing to the ledger.

GenAI suggests the rules, with a quick add.

Granular control of what auto publishes. E.g. only rules on this specific account.

GenAI identifies FX invoice matches & bank transfers.

Wrapping up

Key takeaways.

01

It won’t replace us.

Not as claimed, anyway.It’s being oversold.

02

Probabilistic, not intelligent.

Not correct every time.It hallucinates by design.

03

Copilot, never the pilot.

Be wary of autonomous end to end agentic GenAI for accounting work.

04

Use it every day.

Chat as a daily copilot.Build tools with Claude Code.Use tools like AI dictation to augment workflow.(It’s still incredible tech.)
