# AI from the Front Line: AI in accounting

The evolving deck for sessions with firms' teams. Working title, Dave's words.

Live at `/ai-in-accounting`, which redirects to this folder. 94 slides: 40 for the session, then a Backup divider and 53 backup slides (the narrative, the science in depth, the app screens).

Structured around the forms of AI an accountant will meet. The map slide (9) lists all seven with a verdict each, and comes back before every section with that section highlighted.

Built on 23 November 2026 from Auto Bank Wreck (the base, its look and its notes) and AI Native is a Lie. Edit `index.html` directly from here on. Images this deck uses were copied into `images/` (AI Native ones under `images/from-ai-native/`), so the two source decks can change without breaking this one.
